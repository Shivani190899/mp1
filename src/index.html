Clean Water Act Compliance Dashboard

This project explores how environmental noncompliance varies across states and industries, and how inspection and enforcement activity relates to those patterns.

Explore the four dashboard views

  • Geographic hotspots: compare violations and the duration of noncompliance across states.
  • Industry patterns: explore industry-level violations, severity, and changes over time.
  • Inspection activity: review inspections by quarter, agency, and inspection type.
  • Enforcement and risk: compare inspections and enforcement actions with noncompliance patterns.

The dashboard brings these views together to help identify areas for closer investigation.
